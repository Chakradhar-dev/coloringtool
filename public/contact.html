Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is ColoringTool.com 100% free?

Yes! All 100+ drawings, interactive coloring tools, and printables are completely free without any subscriptions or paid locks.

Can teachers print drawings for school classrooms?

Absolutely! We provide an open educational license for teachers, daycare centers, and libraries to print and photocopy drawings for in-class learning activities.

Do you collect personal information from children?

No. In accordance with COPPA regulations, we never collect personal identifying information from children under 13. Access is 100% anonymous.
